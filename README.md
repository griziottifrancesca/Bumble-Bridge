
# Bumble Bridge

**Real-time audio translation for cross-border SME meetings, built into Zoom and Microsoft Teams.**

Bumble Bridge is a venture concept developed for **ENTRE 370 (Venture Development)** at the **University of Washington Foster School of Business** (Spring 2026). It started from one question: *why do so many international business meetings end with half the room staying silent?*

📊 **Pitch deck:** [View on Canva](https://canva.link/spvkuujofqjk0js)

---

## The problem

Small and mid-sized companies increasingly sell, source and partner across borders, but most can't afford interpreters for every call. As a result:

- Non-native speakers participate less in meetings, even when they hold critical knowledge.
- Deals and partnerships slow down because of misunderstandings and follow-up clarification.
- Existing tools mostly offer **text subtitles**, which force participants to read instead of engaging with the speaker.

Large enterprises have interpreters and dedicated localisation teams. SMEs usually have neither.

## The solution

Bumble Bridge is an **audio-first** plug-in that translates speech in real time directly inside the video-conferencing tools companies already use.

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Audio-first translation** | Each participant hears the conversation in their own language, instead of reading subtitles |
| **Native integration** | Works as a plug-in for **Zoom** and **Microsoft Teams**, with no new platform to adopt |
| **Business-ready** | Designed for sales calls, supplier negotiations and cross-border team meetings |
| **CRM integration** (Pro tier) | Connects meeting outputs to the company's sales workflow |

## Target market

- **Primary segment:** SMEs with **50–500 employees** in **technology, software and industrial goods**
- **Geography:** U.S.-based SMEs working with international partners, and foreign SMEs entering the U.S. market
- **Market sizing:** TAM/SAM/SOM analysis of the global AI-translation market (~$2.3B today, projected at $8–12B by 2030)

## Customer validation

We ran **primary interviews with five executives and managers** from SMEs and mid-market companies in the U.S. and Italy, including C-level profiles (CEO, CFO, CISO) from manufacturing and IT-services firms.

Key takeaways:

- The language barrier is a real, recurring cost in cross-border operations.
- **Data security** is a gating concern for IT and security leaders, so it shaped our positioning.
- Two interviewees indicated a **willingness to pay of about $100 setup + $10–20/month**, which set the floor for our pricing.

## Business model

SaaS subscription with two tiers:

| Tier | What's included |
|---|---|
| **Starter** | Up to 3 hours of translated meetings per month, then $0.30/min overage |
| **Pro** | Unlimited translation + CRM integration |

**Competitive benchmarking:** Transync ($24.99), DeepL Voice ($57.49), Maestra ($79), Jeenie ($1.99/min).

**Unit economics:** ~$0.13/min variable cost using third-party APIs, falling to an estimated $0.05–0.07/min with a self-hosted model.

## Go-to-market

- Direct outreach to SMEs actively expanding across borders
- Distribution through the **Zoom App Marketplace** and **Microsoft AppSource**
- Demand creation through content and partnerships with export and trade networks
